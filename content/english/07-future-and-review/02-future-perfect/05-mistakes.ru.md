# Частые ошибки

```mistake
By Friday I will finished the project.
By Friday I will have finished the project.
Без have время не строится: will have finished.

I'll have finish the project by Friday.
I'll have finished the project by Friday.
После have — третья форма: finished.

She will has finished by six.
She will have finished by six.
После will — всегда have, даже с she.

I'll have finished the project until Friday.
I'll have finished the project by Friday.
«К пятнице, не позже» — by. until — «вплоть до», всё время до.

By the time you will arrive, we'll have left.
By the time you arrive, we'll have left.
После by the time — Present Simple, как после when.

We'll have went home by then.
We'll have gone home by then.
went — вторая форма. После have — третья: gone.
```

> **Подсказка:** видишь by, by the time или by then и речь о том, что будет готово, — ставь
> will have + третья форма.
