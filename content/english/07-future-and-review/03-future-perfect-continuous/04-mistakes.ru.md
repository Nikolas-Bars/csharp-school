# Частые ошибки

```mistake
By June I will have been study English for a year.
By June I will have been studying English for a year.
После have been глагол нужен с -ing: studying.

By June I will been studying English for a year.
By June I will have been studying English for a year.
Пропущен have: will have been.

By June I will have been studying English since a year.
By June I will have been studying English for a year.
Год — срок, а не точка. Со сроком — for.

By May I will have been knowing Emma for five years.
By May I will have known Emma for five years.
know — глагол состояния, -ing с ним не ставят. Нужен Future Perfect.

By Friday I will have been writing three essays.
By Friday I will have written three essays.
Три сочинения — сколько сделано, результат. С числом — Future Perfect.
```

> **Подсказка:** видишь момент в будущем и срок с for — скорее всего, это Future Perfect
> Continuous: will have been + -ing.
