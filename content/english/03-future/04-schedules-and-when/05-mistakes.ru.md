# Частые ошибки

```mistake
When I will get home, I'll call you.
When I get home, I'll call you.
После when о будущем — Present Simple. will — только в главной части.

If it will rain, we'll stay home.
If it rains, we'll stay home.
После if — Present Simple, а с it к глаголу нужно -s: rains.

I'll text you as soon as I will be ready.
I'll text you as soon as I'm ready.
После as soon as — Present Simple: I'm ready.

If you won't hurry, you'll be late.
If you don't hurry, you'll be late.
Отрицание после if — тоже Present Simple: don't hurry.

What time the train leaves?
What time does the train leave?
Вопрос в Present Simple строится с does, а глагол после него — без -s.
```
